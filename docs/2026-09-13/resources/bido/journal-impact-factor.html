journal impact factor
ni

IRI: http://purl.org/spar/bido/journal-impact-factor

comment
A measure reflecting the average number of citations to recent articles published in the journal. It is frequently used as a proxy for the relative importance of a journal within its field, with journals with higher impact factors deemed to be more important than those with lower ones.
